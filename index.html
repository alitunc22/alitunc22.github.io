Shorts Otomasyonu

Shorts Otomasyonu is a private desktop application used by its owner to publish short educational videos about animals (in Spanish) to the owner's own YouTube channels.

What the app does

  • Creates short vertical videos with narration and subtitles.
  • Uploads the videos to YouTube channels that belong to the app owner, using the YouTube Data API.
  • Changes the visibility of those videos (from private to public) at a scheduled time.

The app is not offered to the public. Only the owner's own Google accounts sign in to it.
