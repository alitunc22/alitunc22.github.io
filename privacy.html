Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

This policy explains how Shorts Otomasyonu ("the app") handles information when its owner connects YouTube channels to it.

Who uses the app

The app is a private tool. It is used only by its owner to manage the owner's own YouTube channels. It is not offered to other people.

Information the app accesses

When a YouTube channel is connected, the app requests permission through Google OAuth to:

  • upload videos to that channel;
  • read and update the title, description, tags and visibility of videos the app uploaded;
  • read the channel's name so the owner can recognise it in the app.

The app does not access viewers' data, comments, subscribers' personal information, payment information or any data outside the connected YouTube channels.

How the information is used

The information is used only to upload the owner's videos and to make them public at the scheduled time. It is not used for advertising, profiling or any other purpose.

Storage and sharing

  • Access tokens are stored by Composio (composio.dev), the service the app uses to connect to YouTube, and are used only to perform the actions listed above.
  • Video information (titles, upload times, video IDs) is stored locally on the owner's computer.
  • No information is sold or shared with third parties.

Google API Services User Data Policy

The app's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. The app uses YouTube API Services; see the Google Privacy Policy.

Removing access

Access can be revoked at any time from Google Account → Security → Third-party apps. After that, the app can no longer access the channel.
